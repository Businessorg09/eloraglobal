What is Elora Global and how does it work?
Elora Global combines structured trading education, skill-based qualification, funded trading pathways, advanced trading tools, and ongoing trader development in one ecosystem.
How does the Elora qualification process work?
You progress through the required learning modules and complete MCQ-based assessments. After completing the required phases and passing the final qualification, you can unlock the funding pathway associated with your package.
What funded account can I unlock?
Your funding pathway depends on your Elora Global package: Starter → $10K, Growth → $25K, and Elite → $100K.
What is included in the Trading Engine?
The Trading Engine brings together your academy, qualification center, trading dashboard, trading tools, journal, analytics, strategy tools, market resources, community, and package-specific features.
What is the difference between Starter, Growth, and Elite?
Each package provides a different level of education, coaching, tools, and trading development. Starter focuses on learning and qualification, Growth adds advanced development and weekly coaching, while Elite adds intensive coaching, live market sessions, live trading education, and advanced tools.
Can I learn and trade from mobile?
Yes. The Elora Trading Engine is designed to provide a seamless experience across desktop and mobile devices, allowing you to access your education, tools, journal, analytics, and trading environment wherever you are.
